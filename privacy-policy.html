Privacy Policy

Important information about using this website and communicating with Zemuco.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This policy explains how Zemuco Havilah Real Estate Company Ltd handles information provided through this website and related enquiry channels.

Information you submit

You may provide your name, phone number, email address, property or project location, service interest, message and preferred contact method. Avoid sending confidential title documents or sensitive identity records through ordinary enquiry channels.

How enquiry information is used

Information is used to understand and respond to your enquiry, communicate about requested property services and maintain appropriate business records. Direct WhatsApp and email actions transfer you to the chosen service; this website does not store an enquiry in a database.

Communications

Zemuco may respond using the contact method you select. You can ask not to receive further non-essential communication.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently set a custom analytics or advertising cookie. Hosting providers and third-party resources may process limited technical information. If analytics are enabled later, this policy should be updated and appropriate notice provided.

Third-party tools

WhatsApp, email applications, Google Maps, content delivery networks and hosting providers operate under their own privacy terms. When you follow an external link, your use of that service is governed by its provider.

Retention and security

Business enquiry information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for communication, service administration and applicable record-keeping. Reasonable care is appropriate, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

External links

This website may link to external services. Zemuco is not responsible for their content, security or privacy practices.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as website practices change. The revised date shown on this page indicates the latest published version.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@zemuco.ng or addressed to Zemuco Havilah Real Estate Company Ltd, Opposite Gold Value Hotel, 191 Chime Avenue, New Haven, Enugu, Nigeria.
