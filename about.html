Who we are

Property support grounded in clear information and coordinated action

Zemuco Havilah Real Estate Company Ltd is a Nigerian real-estate company based in New Haven, Enugu.

We support land buyers, property owners, investors, developers, landlords, businesses and organisations across property acquisition, development coordination, verification, documentation, management, advisory, marketing and site planning.

Every engagement begins with the client’s actual property objective. We avoid unsupported promises and help identify where verification, clearer records, structured communication or relevant specialist involvement may be necessary.

Our approach

A structured path that remains responsive to the client

The exact steps depend on the property matter, but our working approach is built around five practical ideas.

  1. Understand

    Begin with the client’s objective, circumstances and available information.

  2. Assess

    Identify the property questions, constraints and checks relevant to the matter.

  3. Coordinate

    Bring activities, communication and necessary professional inputs into clearer order.

  4. Support

    Assist through the agreed property-related actions and decisions.

  5. Communicate

    Maintain clear, client-focused updates throughout the scope.

Our commitment

Greater clarity, structure and professional support

Our direction is to help individuals, investors and organisations approach property decisions with clearer information and a more coordinated process.
