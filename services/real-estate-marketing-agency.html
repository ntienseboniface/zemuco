Real Estate Marketing & Agency Services

Bringing a property to market requires accurate information, considered presentation, responsive enquiry handling and coordinated communication. Zemuco supports owners through these practical marketing and agency activities.

Service overview

Effective marketing starts with a property that can be clearly presented

Bringing a property to market requires accurate information, considered presentation, responsive enquiry handling and coordinated communication. Zemuco supports owners through these practical marketing and agency activities.

  • Accurate, supportable property information
  • An appropriate presentation for the intended audience
  • A considered marketing and enquiry strategy
  • Organised viewing and lead communication
  • Clear coordination as serious interest develops
How we can assist

Organised support around your objective

Zemuco begins with the circumstances of the property and the outcome the client is working towards. The service scope may include:

  • Reviewing the property information available for marketing
  • Supporting the preparation and presentation of marketing material
  • Coordinating enquiries from prospective buyers or tenants
  • Organising property viewing communication
  • Maintaining transaction communication with relevant parties
Professional boundary: Marketing outcomes depend on the property, information, market conditions, pricing decisions and other circumstances. Zemuco does not guarantee a sale, lease, price, timeframe or transaction outcome.
When this may be useful

Common client situations

  • You have property you are preparing to sell or lease
  • Enquiries need a consistent local coordination point
  • Property information needs to be organised before marketing
  • You live outside Nigeria and need support handling local interest
  • Marketing activity needs to connect with documentation or management
A suggested process

Clear stages, adapted to the matter

  1. Review

    Understand the property and confirm the marketing objective.

  2. Prepare

    Organise relevant information and presentation materials.

  3. Market

    Use the agreed channels to present the property.

  4. Coordinate

    Handle enquiries and organise viewing communication.

  5. Progress

    Support clear communication as qualified interest develops.
